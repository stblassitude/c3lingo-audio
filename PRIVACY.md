# Privacy Policy

This policy covers the c3lingo audio apps for iOS, iPadOS, macOS, visionOS and Android
("the app"), published on the App Store, Google Play and F-Droid.

Responsible for the app: Stefan Bethke, Arndtstr. 11, 22085 Hamburg

## In short

The app doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no
advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting. It never uses the
microphone or the camera.

## What the app sends, and to whom

To show the channels and play audio, the app connects to one server: by default
`https://audio.c3lingo.org/`, operated by c3lingo, or a server you entered yourself in
the app's server settings. It sends nothing to anyone else.

- **The web page** with the channel list, the schedule and the talk information is
  loaded from that server, like a web browser would.
- **The audio** is streamed from that server with WebRTC. To set up the connection, the
  app tells the server the channel you chose and the network addresses it can be reached
  at, and joins with a random name that is made up for each connection.

Like any server on the internet, the server sees your IP address and the requests the app
makes. Access logs are kept for only 8 hours.
The app doesn't send any information that identifies you, your device or your account.

Links to other sites, such as a talk's page in the schedule, open in your web browser,
where that site's privacy policy applies.

## What the app stores on your device

- the server URL, if you changed it
- what the web page stores for itself, such as the language you chose

This stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app. Changing the server
deletes everything the web page stored.
