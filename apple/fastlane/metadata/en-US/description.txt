Listen to c3lingo's live interpretation and the hall audio of talks at the Chaos Communication Congress and other events, on your phone and with your own headphones.

The app shows the event's channel list from the server, so it always matches the current event. Playback is native:

• keeps playing in the background and with the screen locked
• controls on the lock screen, in Control Center and on headphones
• AirPlay and Bluetooth output
• reconnects automatically after network changes and interruptions such as phone calls
• never uses the microphone, and plays as media, not as a call

For iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro.

The audio is streamed with WebRTC from a Galène server (https://galene.org/). The app connects to https://audio.c3lingo.org/, which can be changed in the settings.

The app is open source: https://github.com/stblassitude/c3lingo-audio
