• New: subtitles. While a channel plays, the subtitles button shows what is said as text, transcribed on your device in the channel's language
• The first time for a language, your device downloads its speech recognition, and the subtitles start by themselves once it is ready
• iOS: Subtitles need iOS or iPadOS 26 or later
• macOS: Subtitles need macOS 26 or later
• Android: Subtitles need Android 13 or later and on-device speech recognition, which most recent phones have
